Get LED Wall Creator

Pick your computer. The download is one file, and the steps below take about a minute. You only do this once: after that, the app updates itself.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · 213 MB

Download for Windows
  1. Open your Downloads folder, right-click LEDWallCreator-windows.zip and choose Extract All.
  2. Move the extracted LEDWallCreator folder wherever you like (Documents is fine).
  3. Open it and double-click LEDWallCreator.exe.
  4. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This happens once, because the app is free and not signed with a paid certificate.

macOS

Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · 172 MB

Download for macOS
  1. Double-click LEDWallCreator-macos.zip in Downloads to unzip it.
  2. Drag LEDWallCreator into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't check the developer: click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. (On older macOS: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open.)
  4. If it ever says the app "is damaged", open Terminal and paste xattr -cr /Applications/LEDWallCreator.app once.

Linux

64-bit (x86-64), Ubuntu 22.04 or similar · 326 MB

Download for Linux
  1. Extract it: right-click → Extract Here, or tar -xzf LEDWallCreator-linux.tar.gz.
  2. Move the LEDWallCreator folder wherever you like.
  3. Run LEDWallCreator/LEDWallCreator (double-click it, or from a terminal).
  4. From then on it's in your applications menu as LED Wall Creator.
Why the warnings? Windows and macOS warn about any app that isn't signed with a paid developer certificate. LED Wall Creator is free, so it isn't. The app only uses the internet to check for updates, and you can turn that off in Help → Check for Updates.
Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no watermark.

What does it make, and how do I play it at the show?

An MP4 video (H.264), by default 3840×1080 for a wide LED wall, or any size you choose for a projector or a screen. Play it from a laptop or media server the way you'd play any video: VLC, QLab, Resolume, or whatever your venue uses. Turn on "Seamless loop" for a clip that repeats without a jump.

What computer do I need?

Any reasonably recent Windows, Mac (Apple Silicon) or Linux computer. A graphics card makes exports a lot faster, but isn't needed.

How do updates work?

When a new version comes out, the app asks you when it starts. One click downloads it, installs it next to the current one, restarts, and removes the old version. Your projects are never touched.

Where are my projects saved?

In Documents/LED Wall Creator by default, one folder per song with its audio and automatic backups, so you can copy a whole show to a USB stick.

I have an Intel Mac.

The ready-made Mac download is for Apple Silicon (M1 and newer). On an Intel Mac you can still run it from its source code with Python. The project's source code page explains how.
