Your band's name, glowing on cue.

Type your name, pick a font and a layout, and decide when it shows. A Band Name Light lane makes it glow and flash on the hits.

How it works

In the app

  1. In Elements, select ★ Band name at the top of the list and type the name.
  2. Pick one of the bundled fonts (they look the same on every computer) or any font you have installed, then the layout, letter case and colour.
  3. Tick Always on to keep it lit the whole song, or bring it in with notes on the Global Band Name lane.
  4. Put notes on the Band Name Light lane to make it glow: short notes flash, long notes glow.
  5. Dark cut-out turns the name into a silhouette against the light behind it.
