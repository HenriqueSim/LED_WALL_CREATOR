Build your own shapes.

When the ready-made shapes aren't enough, combine lines, dashed lines, rectangles, circles, arcs, rays and polygons into one element. Each piece becomes a part the band can light.

How it works

In the app

  1. In Elements, click Add → Custom.
  2. Use Add shape to add pieces: a line, dashes, a rectangle, a circle, an arc, rays or a polygon.
  3. Select a piece to change its size, angle, number of rays or dashes, or drag it in the preview.
  4. Scale, move, rotate or spin the whole element with the sliders at the top.
  5. Each piece is one part: in the Pattern Editor they're shared out between the members, in list order.
