How it works

In the app

  1. In Export, pick the resolution (3840×1080 for a wide LED wall, 1920×1080, or a custom size) and 30 or 60 fps.
  2. The Range is the loop region from the Master Track, or the whole song if there isn't one.
  3. Leave Encode on the graphics card on: the Encoder line shows what your computer will use.
  4. Tick Mux audio to put the song in the video too.
  5. Click Render Video and choose where to save the MP4.
