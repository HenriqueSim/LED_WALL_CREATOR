Light that fills the room.

Every lit part can spread a soft haze into the air around it, so lighting one dash of a beam lights the room around that dash. A separate Haze element washes the stage with colour.

How it works

In the app

  1. Select an element and raise Haze around it in its Light settings: one fader sets how far the haze spreads and how bright it is.
  2. Glow sets the bloom right around the shape.
  3. Add a Haze element for a soft wash of the song colour rising from the bottom. Put notes on its lane where you want it; the velocity sets how much.
  4. In the Global lanes, Flash Peak gives a burst on a hit and Blackout drops the wall to black.
