How it works

In the app

  1. Load your song in Look & Song (WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC…). The tempo and the first beat are detected.
  2. If the grid is slightly off, drag the waveform until bar 1 sits on the first beat, or use the Bar 1 at nudges and Tap at the bottom.
  3. Double-click a track to place the selected pattern; drag clips to move them and drag their right edge to repeat a pattern for longer.
  4. Shift-drag on the ruler to set a loop region, and tick Seamless loop for a clip that repeats without a jump.
  5. Double-click the Lyrics lane to add a line of lyrics at that moment.
