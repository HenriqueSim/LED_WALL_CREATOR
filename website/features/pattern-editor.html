Draw light like you'd program a beat.

Every shape on the wall has a folder of lanes: the whole shape, one lane per band member, one per group, and the motion effects. Draw notes on a 1/16 grid while the section loops.

How it works

In the app

  1. Pick a pattern on the left (or click New). A pattern is a loop of 1 to 16 bars.
  2. Open an element's folder and drag across a lane to draw a note: its length is how long it's lit, the Velocity box sets how bright.
  3. Use the member and group lanes to light only part of a shape, for example the left side of the stage on the first beat and the right side on the second.
  4. Add Pulse, Chase, Random or Strobe for motion, and set Effect speed to ½× or 2× for half-time or double-time sections.
  5. Playback loops the part of the song you're editing, so you see the result straight away.
