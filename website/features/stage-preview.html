How it works

In the app

  1. Open the Export tab and press Play from the start.
  2. Switch View to On stage: LED wall: the wall lights the band from behind.
  3. Or pick On stage: projector: the picture comes from the front, lands on the band, and they cast shadows on the screen.
  4. Tick House lights to add the venue's moving lights, the ones you don't control, and set how strong they are.
  5. Open big preview window and press F11 for fullscreen, to show the band.
