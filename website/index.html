Pattern Editor

Draw light like you'd program a beat.

Every shape on the wall gets its own lanes, for the whole shape, for each band member and for groups like "left side". Draw notes on a 1/16 grid: their length is how long it's lit, their velocity is how bright. Add Pulse, Chase, Random or Strobe, at half or double time for a section.

1/16 gridMembers & groupsPulse · Chase · Random · Strobe½× – 4× speed
Elements

Simple shapes that breathe with the music.

A sunset sun, a horizontal beam, a hexagon, vertical blocks, light shafts. Or build your own from lines, arcs, rays and polygons. Every lit part spreads a soft haze into the room around it, and a haze layer rises and falls with the song.

Master Track

Locked to your song.

Load your track and the tempo is detected for you. Line up bar 1 by dragging the waveform, then arrange your patterns along the song like clips in a music app: groove, breakdown, drop. Loop any part while you work on it.

Tempo detectionTap tempoClips & tracksSeamless loops
Your name in lights

Your band's name, glowing on cue.

Type the name, pick a font and a layout, and keep it on all night or bring it in for the chorus. A Band Name Light lane makes it flash on the hits. A dark cut-out style turns it into a silhouette against the light. Lyrics get their own lane on the master track.

Watch the whole video behind a simulated band, one silhouette per member, with an audience in front. Switch between an LED wall that backlights the band and a projector that lights them from the front. Add the venue's own moving lights to see how it all plays together.
